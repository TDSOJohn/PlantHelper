            Temperature and height match a recorded figure at or below what you type — the coldest an entry is known to take, the tallest it is known to get. pH matches a recorded range that covers the figure. The three marks come from what the article says, so unticked means it went unmentioned rather than that the answer is no. An entry that records nothing matches nothing, and most record nothing.
